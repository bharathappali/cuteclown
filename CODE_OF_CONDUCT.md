# Code of Conduct

CuteClown is a community project. People contributing issues, reviews, and code are expected to treat one another with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent and keep criticism on the work.
- Make room for questions, especially about accessibility and inclusive design.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or sexualized language and imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using the contact listed on the GitHub profile for this repository. Reports are reviewed as quickly as practical. Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove participants who break this policy.

This policy applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.
